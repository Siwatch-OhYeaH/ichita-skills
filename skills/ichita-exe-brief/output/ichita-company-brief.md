# ICHITA Co., Ltd. — Company Brief

**Prepared by**: ICHITA Thailand | **Date**: March 2026 | **Classification**: General

## Executive Summary

ICHITA is a Thai-based Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) specializing in separation technologies, water treatment, and process engineering solutions. With expertise spanning chromatographic purification, decolorization, and membrane filtration, ICHITA serves clients across food & beverage, pharmaceutical, and industrial sectors.

## Core Capabilities

| Capability | Description | Key Markets |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Chromatographic Separation** | SMB and batch chromatography for sugar purification, amino acids, and specialty chemicals | Food & Beverage, Pharma |
| **Decolorization Systems** | Activated carbon and ion exchange resin systems for color removal | Sugar mills, Palm oil |
| **Membrane Filtration** | UF, NF, and RO systems for water treatment and process concentration | Industrial, Municipal |
| **Process Engineering** | End-to-end process design, simulation, and optimization | All sectors |

## Key Differentiators

- **Vertical Integration**: Design, manufacture, install, and commission — all in-house
- **Thai Engineering Expertise**: Local team with deep understanding of ASEAN industrial needs
- **Innovation Focus**: Active R&D in rare sugars (Allulose), functional ingredients, and green chemistry
- **Cost Efficiency**: Thai manufacturing base with competitive pricing vs. European/Japanese OEMs

## Strategic Initiatives

### 1. Rare Sugar Hub

Thailand's first Allulose production facility — targeting US, EU, Japan, and Korea export markets. Leveraging Thai cassava supply chain and proprietary enzymatic conversion technology.

### 2. ASEAN Expansion

Establishing regional partnerships in Vietnam, Indonesia, and Philippines for water treatment solutions. Target: 3 new country offices by 2027.

### 3. Green Process Innovation

Developing low-energy separation processes using novel membrane materials. Goal: 30% reduction in energy consumption per unit of purified product by 2028.
