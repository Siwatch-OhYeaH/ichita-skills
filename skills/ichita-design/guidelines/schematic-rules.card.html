A figure is not a drawing of the plant; it is an argument about the plant. These rules are what keep one legible from the back of a room — adapted from the editorial diagram grammar, re-set in ICHITA type sizes, ICHITA scope colour and ICHITA's square-cornered geometry.

Colour does one job per figure

Ichita Blue marks ICHITA scope. A secondary hue marks a technology family — cyan membrane, bronze ion exchange, rose adsorbent, violet chromatography — and only one of them may appear on a canvas, with a legend. Functional colour marks state: Success within spec, Warning threshold, Error off-spec, Attention the milestone or callout. Never mix the two vocabularies, never set type in a core, and never colour the product stream — it is the output of all four families, so it stays Ichita Blue or Blue Grey.
