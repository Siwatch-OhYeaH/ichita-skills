Brand Guidelines V1.0 — 6.0 Photography

Photography

Our photography focuses around the use of two defined image types, People and Operational, each reflecting a different aspect of the ICHITA offering, process and values. Everything is shot cool and unfiltered — steel, water and the brand blue carry the colour.

6.1 People

Position us as a company focused on our customers and our workforce, and the close relationship between them. Personal, relatable and reliable — as well as assertive and confident in our industry.

Do shoot people mid-task, in correct PPE, in a working environment. Candid framing, natural expression.
Don't use stock handshakes, boardroom smiles, hi-vis models on white, or anyone in PPE that the site would not permit.

6.1 Operational

Communicates and contextualises the scale of the ICHITA process. Wider shots show the factory; more detailed close-ups visualise our attention to detail and expertise.

Scale — wide

Detail — close up

Do shoot in the plant's own light, cool white balance, steel and shadow. Long lenses for compression, shallow depth for detail.
Don't add warm or teal-orange grades, HDR halos, lens flare, or composite pipework that could not exist.

Materials Extension

Not in Guidelines V1.0. Added for datasheets and product pages, where the media itself is the subject. Same cool, unfiltered treatment; seamless graduated background rather than a plant setting.

Food-grade and potable applications: the sample must look clean enough to eat beside. No fingerprints, no scale, no scratched acrylic, no dusty benches.

Treatment

How an image behaves once it is on a page or a slide.

Case study — Brine recovery
Text goes on the dark end of the frame, never over the subject.
Scrim, don't tint. A linear Blue Black scrim from 88% to 0% across the frame. Keep at least 4.5:1 behind every character of text.
Crop, don't stretch. Full bleed or flush to the grid column. Square, 4:3 and 16:9 only; no rounded corners, no drop shadows, no borders.
Duotone is for pattern, not people. Blue Grey 03 → Blue duotone is permitted on detail and scale shots used as texture behind a section marker. Never duotone a face.
Never place the logo over a busy area, apply the brand pattern on top of a photograph, or use a photograph as a full-page background behind body copy.

The palette is already in the photography: Blue Black shadow, Blue Grey steel, brand Blue in the instrument lines. If a shot does not sit on this strip, it is the wrong shot — grade it back, don't push it further.
