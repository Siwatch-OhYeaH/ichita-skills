Presentation method

The ICHITA deck arc

The house narrative, as ICHITA presents it. Reference build: the Overcome Challenges in Sugar Decolorization with Ecosorb Technologies webinar. Not every deck needs all fifteen acts; the order does not change. Earn attention with a question, promise the benefit before naming the subject, agree on the process the room already runs, put a cost on its weak point, then answer with mechanism, technique and measured evidence.

01

Hook — a question

One question over a full-bleed photograph, keywords in accent colour. No agenda, no logo wall. "If fine liquor colour held at 100–150 IU all the time, what would the refinery gain?"

Answer it — benefits first

Repeat the question small at the top, then list what the audience gets. Five lines, each a gain, marked in Success green. The subject has still not been named.

Topics

A plain short list — five items, no decoration — plus how long the session runs. Brief by design; it is a signpost, not a chapter.

05

Name the challenges

The same diagram, and beside it the numbered list of what goes wrong — four is the working number. This list is a contract: act 12 answers it item for item.

06

One slide per challenge

Same diagram again, once per item. Degraded figures in Error red at the stage that fails, and the consequence spelled out: yield decreases · higher operating cost.

07

Divider

Dark full field, the section title as a spoken line — "How ECOSORB works". One before each major act: solution, technique, application, references.

08

Credibility

Who stands behind the product — parent group, manufacturing sites, certifications and compliance. Placed before the technical claim, never after it.

09

What it is

The product stated plainly in two sentences, with the media photographed. Composition and principle only; performance comes next.

03-content
10

A benefit per slide

The headline is the benefit, not the feature — "Improved filterability", "No carbon leakage" — with the mechanism diagram, photo or curve as its evidence. A feature list alone reads as selling.

03-content · 10-comparison
11

Operating technique

Numbered modes (precoat · body feed · hybrid), each a schematic plus its dosing profile, the active mode picked out in Attention orange. Close with a three-up "which one for your case".

08-process-flow · 10-comparison
12

Application — close the loop

One case per challenge named in act 05, in the same order, plus one they did not ask for. Same diagram each time, the modification at a different position; before in red, after in green.

08-process-flow
13

References

A real plant per slide: photograph, duty and capacity, the flow as built, and an operating table reading before → after. End on the payback line — "benefit is 2× cost, ~375,000 USD/year".

14

Bookend — hand it back

Full-bleed plant photograph and the opening question turned on the audience: "How would you like to improve your refinery?" The deck closes the loop it opened.

15

Thanks and Q&A

Dark field, one line, the marks. A separate quiet Q&A slide to hold the room. No recap of what was already said.

06-closing
One diagram carries the whole deck. Acts 04, 05, 06, 11 and 12 are the same process drawing, changed in one place each time — see The carried diagram.
