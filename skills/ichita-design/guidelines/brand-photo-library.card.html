Photo library

Forty-one brand photographs, moved out of uploads/, renamed by subject and filed in assets/imagery/. One consistent look throughout — cool blue-grey, deep shadow, stainless and blue tubing — so any two of them sit together on a spread. Most are 2500–5500 px on the long edge, so unlike the old set these are full-bleed material. Crop with object-fit:cover; never stretch, never round the corners.

Add a photograph

Using them

The treatment rules on the Photography card apply unchanged. These are about editing and honesty.

Naming is the filing system. people-, detail-, aerial-, site-, product-, water- — subject first, qualifier after, kebab case. A new photograph without one of those prefixes is not in the library.
One per surface. These share a single grade, so two on a spread reads as a pattern, three as wallpaper. Pair a wide frame with a detail, not with a second wide.
Scrim before type. Dark ground plus white type only over the deep frames, and always through a scrim — the water and aerial shots carry too much local contrast for bare text.
Not evidence. This is brand imagery, not the reference set: no frame here is a named ICHITA delivery. A case study, reference plant or FIG. caption needs a real site photograph with plant, duty and year.

Originals stay in uploads/ (SRYA_Imagery_*, SRYA_Process_Landscapes_*, SRYA_Product_*, Water Image *) — the copies here are the library. The dark cover artwork and the slide content frame live separately in assets/brand/. This card shows 600 px proxies from assets/imagery/thumbs/ so it loads instantly — always link the full-size file in assets/imagery/ from a slide or document, never the proxy.
