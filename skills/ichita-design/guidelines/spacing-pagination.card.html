Decide by document length
Long — proposal, manual, textbook, tender (8+ pp)
  • New chapter, new page. Always — even if the last one ended two lines down.
  • The opener carries its number, title and lead, and nothing that could have started earlier.
  • Front matter and each appendix get their own page.
Sheet 2 ends short — correct, not a defect
Short — report, memo, datasheet (2–6 pp)
  • A section may continue on the page it starts; two sections on one sheet is correct here.
  • There is no long-range navigation to protect, so the trade is allowed.
Never split these
  • A table or figure and its caption
  • A heading and the first two lines under it
  • A lead-in sentence and its list
  • A callout, pull quote or metadata grid
  • A row of a comparison table
  • Fewer than two lines of a paragraph either side of the break
Enforce with break-inside:avoid on the group — never by nudging margins
If it won't fit — in this order
  1. Move the whole block to the next page and let this one end short.
  2. Cut copy.
  3. Re-flow to two columns if the content suits it.
  4. Split at a natural sub-head and carry a running head over.
Never to buy a line
Shrink the type below its floor · squeeze the leading · reduce the margin · tighten the rhythm ladder. A page ending 40 mm short reads better than one crammed to the edge.
