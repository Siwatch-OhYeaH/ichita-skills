Presentation method

The carried diagram

One process drawing runs the length of the deck. Between slides it changes in exactly one place — a stage that fails, a unit replaced, a stream re-routed — and the figures underneath carry the argument. The listener reads the delta; redrawing the picture throws that away and restarts their comprehension.

Never redraw, re-scale or re-style the diagram between slides — position, size and glyph set are fixed for the whole deck.
One change per slide. Two changes and the audience does not know which one is the point.
Figures live under the glyph they describe, TH Aeonik Bold tabular — never in a legend the eye has to travel to.
Animate the transition, not the build: the previous state is on screen, one element changes. 320 ms, no bounce.
