The rule
  • Break to its own row when the items carry different labels, when any value can wrap, or when there are three or more.
  • Keep in line only when the run reads as one sentence of the same kind — a date, a document number, a byline.
  • Space separates; rules decorate. One hairline under the letterhead and one 3 pt rule under the title. Never a rule above a section head that already has 34 pt of air and a blue bar.
  • Margins are 25 mm all round (the document standard). Never buy space by shrinking the type.
