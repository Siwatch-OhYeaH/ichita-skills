Thai stops following the Latin above 700. Bold, ExtraBold and Black all carry Bai Jamjuree Bold at three embolden amounts — measured, they separate 2.3% in the Thai against 11% in the Latin, and at 900 the Thai stem falls to 0.76 of the Latin against 0.88 through the text weights. 800 and 900 are Latin display weights; do not set a Thai line in them and expect a match.
Every weight has an italic. Three Latin weights — Book 350, SemiBold 600, ExtraBold 800 — are ICHITA interpolations, not CoType drawings; they say so in their own nameID5. All ten hold the same 1.536 line box, so changing weight never reflows a paragraph vertically.
