The declared line box is 1.536 and does not change with weight. Word sets a line from the font's own box, so Single already gives TH Aeonik's 1.536 em and clears every Thai stack; Multiple m gives m × 1.536 em (the 1.75 em Thai body is Multiple 1.14). PowerPoint ignores the font and lays out every line at 1.2 em, so Thai there needs Multiple 1.3 or more (1.536 ÷ 1.2 = 1.28; the 1.75 em body is 1.46). Never Exactly below 1.536 × the point size — it clips the tone marks. Measured in Word and PowerPoint, ichita-skills/docs/THAI-LATIN-FONT-ENGINEERING.md §7. On the web, never inherit line-height:normal: it resolves to 1.536 where TH Aeonik is installed and 1.20 where it is not.
Known gaps
No NBSP in the family (nor thin, figure or narrow space, ZWSP, soft hyphen) — &nbsp; renders from a fallback font, so use a normal space inside .ich-nowrap. Greek is Δ Σ Ω μ π only: α β γ λ σ fall back mid-word. Thai is complete, 87/87. Full audit in fonts.md.
Figures
tnum is present in all 22 faces and holds a digit at ~0.600em in every weight (0.594–0.606), so a figure column keeps its width when the weight changes. Proportional figures move a lot — a "1" is 0.268em at Air and 0.393em at Black. Any figure the reader compares: .ich-figure — Bold, tabular, −0.035em.
