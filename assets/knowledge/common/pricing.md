## Installation Costs (Rule of Thumb)

| Item | Factor | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Mechanical installation | 15-20% of equipment | Rigging, alignment |
| Piping installation | 1.5-2.5x material cost | Labor + fittings |
| Electrical installation | 10-15% of equipment | Cable, conduit, termination |
| Commissioning | 5-8% of total | Startup, testing |
| Engineering & design | 8-12% of total | Detail engineering |
