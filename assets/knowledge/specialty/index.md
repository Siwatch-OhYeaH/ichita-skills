## Nuosep HP — PRIORITY to Populate

Nuosep HP is Ichita's proprietary adsorbent resin for sugar decolorization:
- Regenerable with NaOH
- Competes with IX resin but lower OPEX
- Key differentiator for Ichita proposals
- Data source: internal testing + customer trials
