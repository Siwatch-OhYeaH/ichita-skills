What is in these files
----------------------
Each face is a merge of two sources:

  Latin   Aeonik, (c) 2018 CoType Foundry, licensed to ICHITA under the
          This Is Our Shop end-user licence. Every Latin charstring is
          CoType's own drawing, restored verbatim after the merge, EXCEPT
          the three weights CoType never drew — Book 350, SemiBold 600 and
          ExtraBold 800 — which ICHITA synthesised from the neighbouring
          weight. Those three declare it in their own nameID5 and nameID10.

  Thai    Bai Jamjuree, Copyright 2018 Bai Jamjuree
          (https://github.com/cadsondemak/Bai-Jamjuree), licensed under the
          SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 — see OFL.txt beside this file.
          The outlines are scaled, and in most weights emboldened or thinned,
          to sit on Aeonik's baseline at a matched weight.

Why redistribution is held
--------------------------
OFL 1.1 clause 5 requires that a modified version of OFL font software be
"distributed entirely under this license, and must not be distributed under
any other license". The Aeonik component is under a commercial licence that
does not permit that. A single file cannot satisfy both, so these files are
not distributed at all.

Nothing here restricts USE. OFL clause 2 permits embedding explicitly, and
embedding a font in a PDF is not redistribution of the font software.

If ICHITA ever needs to hand these files to a third party, the EULA-versus-OFL
question has to be resolved by someone with authority to decide it. It is not
resolved by this notice.
