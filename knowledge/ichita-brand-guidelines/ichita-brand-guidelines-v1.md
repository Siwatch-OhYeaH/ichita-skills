## 1. Logo System

### 1.1 Wordmark
- Custom ICHITA™ wordmark
- Main identifier for all communications
- Never alter, redraw, or modify

### 1.2 Symbol
- X-shaped mark made from arrow devices
- Derived from the angle of 'A' in wordmark
- Concept: **Process** & **Innovation**
- Use when ICHITA context is already evident

### 1.3 Clear Zone
- 50% of logo/symbol height
- Nothing should appear inside this area

### 1.4 Logo Colourways
| Background | Logo Color |
|------------|------------|
| White / Blue Grey 01 | Blue Grey 03 (#263338) |
| Ichita Blue | Blue Grey 03 (#263338) |
| Blue Grey 02 | Blue Grey 03 (#263338) |
| Blue Grey 03 / Blue Black | White (#ffffff) |

## 3. Typography

### 3.1 Primary Font: Aeonik
- Timeless and modern aesthetic
- Structural workhorse, meticulously engineered
- Features: ligatures, fractions, case-sensitive punctuation, symbols

| Weight | Usage |
|--------|-------|
| Regular | Body copy, supporting text |
| Medium | Emphasis, subheadings |
| Bold | Headlines, titles |

### 3.2 Display Numbers: Betatron
- Futuristic, mechanical, industrial aesthetic
- Represents innovation and technology
- Use for: large statistics, section numbers, data displays

## 4. Pattern System

### Concept
Based on **"Process"** - creates sense of depth, movement, and dynamism.

### Pattern Styles
1. **Horizontal bars** - varying thickness, fading effect
2. **Vertical bars** - grid-like, industrial feel
3. **Dots/circles** - varying sizes, gradient effect
4. **Combined patterns** - layered for visual interest

### Usage
- Background elements
- Cover designs
- Section dividers
- Can be used at various scales and colors within brand palette

## 5. Illustration

### Technical Illustration Style
- Clean line work
- Consistent stroke weight
- Process equipment: reactors, leaf filters, tanks
- Used for diagrams, charts, technical explanations
- Colors: Blue Grey 03 outlines on light backgrounds

## 6. Photography

### 6.1 People Photography
- Focus on customers and workforce
- Shows close relationships
- Conveys: personal, relatable, reliable, assertive, confident
- Settings: cleanroom, factory floor, office, industrial sites

### 6.2 Operational Photography
- Shows scale of ICHITA process
- Wide shots: factory overview
- Detail shots: attention to detail, expertise
- Equipment close-ups: precision and technology

## 7. Layout Principles

### Document Structure
- Section numbers in corner (e.g., 1.5, 0.9, 1.0)
- Betatron numerals for display (01, 02, 03)
- Logo as sign-off on every page
- Footer: `www.ichita.co.th`
- Page numbers in corner

- Mix of dark and light pages for contrast
- Pattern graphics as backgrounds

- Technical illustrations for process explanation

### Improvement Opportunities

The baseline template is functional but can be enhanced:

1. **Visual Hierarchy** — Add more contrast between title and body
2. **Pattern Integration** — Incorporate brand pattern system (bars, dots)
3. **Color Variety** — Mix light/dark slides per brand guidelines
4. **Statistics Display** — Use Betatron font for big numbers
5. **Modern Layouts** — Add grid layouts, card-based designs
6. **Photo Integration** — Better use of operational/people photography
7. **Whitespace** — More breathing room in content areas

### When Creating Presentations

**DO:**
- Use Aeonik for all text
- Mix dark and light slides for contrast
- Keep logo visible on every slide
- Use Ichita Blue (#2978ff) for accents
- Apply patterns subtly as backgrounds

**DON'T:**
- Overcrowd slides with text
- Use off-brand colors
- Stretch or modify the logo
- Ignore the clear zone rules
