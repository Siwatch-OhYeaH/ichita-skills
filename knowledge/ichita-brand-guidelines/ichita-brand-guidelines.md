## Quick Reference

### Brand in 3 Words
**Innovative. Professional. Precise.**

### Core Colors

| Color | HEX | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| **Ichita Blue** | `#2978ff` | 41, 120, 255 | Vibrant accent, highlights |
| **Blue Light** | `#82b0ff` | 130, 176, 255 | Secondary accent |
| **Blue Grey 01** | `#cfd9db` | 207, 217, 219 | Light backgrounds |
| **Blue Grey 02** | `#788f9c` | 120, 143, 156 | Mid-tone neutral |
| **Blue Grey 03** | `#263338` | 38, 51, 56 | Primary text/logo |
| **Blue Black** | `#171c21` | 23, 28, 33 | Dark backgrounds |
| **White** | `#ffffff` | 255, 255, 255 | Light backgrounds, text on dark |

### Typography

| Font | Weight | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Aeonik** | Bold | Headlines, titles |
| **Aeonik** | Medium | Subheadings |
| **Aeonik** | Regular | Body text |
| **Betatron** | - | Display numerals only (futuristic) |

### Logo Rules

- **Wordmark** = Primary logo (custom ICHITA text)
- **Symbol** = Arrow mark (represents Process + Innovation)
- On light backgrounds: Blue Grey 03
- On dark backgrounds: White
