## 1. Brand Philosophy & Story

### Symbol Meaning
The Ichita Symbol is built from **arrow devices taken from the angle of the 'A' in the Wordmark** around two core concepts:
- **Process** - representing industrial/manufacturing expertise
- **Innovation** - representing forward-thinking technology

The symbol acts as "a representation of the Ichita brand in its most reduced and simplified form."

### Brand Positioning
- Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM)
- Experts in **separation technologies**
- **Water treatment and process solution provider**
- "Process design and engineering experts"

### Core Values
- **Authority & Expertise** - Wordmark reinforces brand authority
- **Process-Driven** - Visual language derives from industrial process
- **Innovation at the Heart** - Betatron font supports technology positioning
- **Customer Focus** - People imagery emphasizes relationships

### Color Psychology
- **Ichita Blue** (#2978ff) - Energy, Innovation, Trust, Vibrancy
- **Blue Grey 03** (#263338) - Authority, Stability, Professionalism
- **Blue Grey 01** (#cfd9db) - Cleanliness, Neutrality, Open
- The palette creates "a dynamic and recognizable mix"

### Usage Rules
- Backgrounds: Primarily White and lighter neutrals
- Darker colors: Used more sparingly
- Logo on light: Blue Grey 03
- Logo on dark: White

## 3. Typography

### Primary Font: Aeonik
- "Timeless and modern aesthetic"
- "A structural workhorse that was meticulously engineered"
- Features: ligatures, fractions, case-sensitive punctuation, symbols, arrows

| Weight | Usage |
|--------|-------|
| **Bold** | Headlines, impact |
| **Medium** | Subheadings, emphasis |
| **Regular** | Body copy, supporting text |

### Display Font: Betatron (Numerals Only)
- Used exclusively for large display numbers
- "Futuristic ideology"
- "Distinctly mechanical and industrial aesthetic"
- Reinforces innovation positioning

## 4. Pattern System

### Concept
Patterns based on **"process"** - connecting to industrial process design and separation technologies.

### 4 Pattern Styles
1. **Horizontal graduated bars** - Thick to thin transition
2. **Vertical bars** - Alternating offset columns
3. **Horizontal parallel lines** - Even spacing, varying thickness
4. **Graduated circles/dots** - Scale transition creating depth

### Usage Guidelines
- Add "versatility and interest"
- Create "a sense of depth and dimension"
- Convey "movement and dynamism"
- Has "a tangible feel"
- Can be used at various scales
- Built to evolve with motion and 3D

## 5. Photography

### Two Image Types

#### People Photography
**Mood**: Personal, Relatable, Reliable, Assertive, Confident

**Visual Style**:
- Workers in cleanroom/protective gear (blue accents)
- Professionals in discussion/collaboration
- Industrial facility settings
- Natural, candid moments
- Blue color accents matching brand

**Purpose**: Position Ichita as customer-focused, relationship-driven

#### Operational/Industrial Photography
**Approach**:
- Wider shots: Show factory scale
- Close-ups: "Visualize attention to detail and expertise"

**Visual Style**:
- Industrial equipment and machinery
- Laboratory/scientific imagery
- Precision instruments, stainless steel
- Clean, sterile environments

## 6. Identity Applied - Presentation Rules

### Title Slides
- **Background**: Blue Grey 03 (#263338) or Ichita Blue (#2978ff)
- **Title**: Mixed styling - first word White, subsequent in Blue accent
- **Logo**: Bottom-right (Wordmark)
- **Section number**: Top-left corner (e.g., "1.5")

### Content Slides
- **Background**: Blue Grey 01 (#cfd9db)
- **Text**: Blue Grey 03
- **Section number**: Top-left
- **Large Betatron numerals**: Bottom-right as visual accent (Blue, outline style)
- **Footer**: Website URL bottom-left

### Cover Designs
**Option A**: White background + vertical bar pattern (Blue Grey 03) + logo bottom-right
**Option B**: Ichita Blue background + horizontal bar pattern + logo bottom-right
**Option C**: Blue Grey 01 + horizontal bars + "Separation Technologies" text

### Case Studies
- Background: Blue Light (#82b0ff)
- Logo: Top-left
- Category label: Top-right
- Large Betatron outline numeral
- Photography: Bottom half, full width

## 7. Overall Brand Vibe

### Brand Personality
**Innovative. Professional. Precise.**

Alternative descriptors:
- Modern, Industrial, Trustworthy
- Technical, Clean, Forward-thinking

### Emotions to Evoke
- **Confidence** - in technical expertise
- **Trust** - in process reliability
- **Innovation** - forward-looking solutions
- **Professionalism** - industrial competence
- **Precision** - attention to detail

### The Balance
- Professional but **Innovative** (Betatron, vibrant blue, dynamic patterns)
- Professional but **Approachable** (people-centric photography, relationships)
- Professional but **Dynamic** (patterns with movement, flexible system)
